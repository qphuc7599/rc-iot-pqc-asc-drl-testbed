is redistributed in this selected artifact. Group-owned harnesses use or cite
PyTorch, NumPy, ns-3, Docker/Compose, liboqs, threshold-ml-dsa, Ganache, ethers,
Solidity and TLA+ tools. These dependencies must be acquired separately under
their own licences; our MIT/CC-BY-4.0 notices do not replace those licences.

The executed threshold dependency is threshold-ml-dsa v0.3.6 from
https://github.com/lattice-safe/threshold-ml-dsa; exact dependency hashes and the
published n<=6 execution envelope remain in the original receipts. It is not
TALUS and not an executed networked 14-of-21 backend.

Published signature timings, standards, historical verifier cost references and
dependency version lists remain attributed in artifact evidence. Citation is
not a grant of rights in the cited works. No publisher-owned manuscript text,
