# Q-Relay virtualised emulation artifact - R1 (v1.1.0)

Public source/data artifact for the minor revision. Source: MIT. Group-owned
data, checkpoints, generated evidence and artifact documentation: CC-BY-4.0.
See LICENSE_SCOPE.md and the exact per-file LICENSE_ASSIGNMENTS.json.

Includes twelve fixed-final A0-A3 checkpoints (three independent training runs
per arm), 240 deterministic paired controller outcomes, 360 inference-only B3
trajectories plus forty reused defaults, frozen stochastic streams, functional
and negative-test evidence, and measured local-EVM ledger exports.

The ablation panel was already observed: all three primary hierarchical
intervals include zero and none is Holm-significant. History removal has a
positive descriptive late-TPS difference. B3 unfavorable settings and CMAB
guard violations remain visible. Integrity PASS is not a safety guarantee.

Node-count effects are analytical only. Withdrawn B4 node sweeps, manuscripts,
cover/response documents and author portraits are excluded. A4/A5, physical
SRAM/stack, enclave execution, native on-chain ML-DSA and public-chain finality
were not evaluated. No new training or simulator/audit rerun was performed.

Source and original/redacted metadata provenance, file SHA256 manifest, licensing
map and prior once-only clone-overlay smoke are supplied. The smoke was not a
new download of this release. Zenodo archiving is triggered by the existing
repository release integration; its actual version DOI is verified separately,
not assumed from the historical concept DOI.
